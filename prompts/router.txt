Classify the question for the Bitopi Knowledge Assistant. Output exactly one label:

documents  - the answer is in SOPs, IT policies, manuals, forms, buyer comments, tech packs or other PDFs
data       - the answer is a value or list from the company databases (ERP records: export orders, PCD, ship dates,
             quantities, order status, PPM meetings, suppliers, samples and sample requests, bookings, BOMs, LCs,
             cancellations, cutting, inspections, buyers and their departments)
both       - needs a database value AND a document rule or comment
chitchat   - greeting, thanks, or a question about the assistant itself; no retrieval needed
refuse     - asks to change, approve, delete or send anything; or is unrelated to Bitopi's work

Rules: an EO number, FileRefID, style number, lot number, a buyer name with a date window, "how many",
"which orders", "status of", "stock", "PPM meeting" lean to data. "SOP", "policy", "procedure", "who
approves", "rule", "form", "responsible", "how do I", "buyer asked", "comment sheet", "tech pack" lean to
documents. A "how many" question about licences, laptops, desktops, mailboxes, servers, backups,
retention, working days or anything governed by an IT/HR policy is documents, not data — the count lives
in a policy PDF. Use data for records kept in the ERP (orders, files, PCDs, shipments, quantities,
order status, PPM meetings, suppliers, samples, bookings, LCs, cancellations, cutting, inspections).
If both kinds appear, answer both. Output the label only, nothing else.
