Classify the question for the Bitopi Knowledge Assistant. Output exactly one label:

documents  - the answer is in SOPs, IT policies, manuals, forms, buyer comments, tech packs or other PDFs
data       - the answer is a value or list from the company databases: ERP records (export orders, PCD, ship
             dates, quantities, order status, PPM meetings, suppliers, samples, bookings, BOMs, LCs,
             cancellations, cutting, inspections, buyers), HR and payroll (employees, headcount, manpower
             budget, attendance, leave, overtime, designations), inventory (GRN, stock, issues, fabric rolls,
             assets), finance (vouchers, payables, invoices, advances, loans, budgets, CNF costs), planning
             and TNA (line plans, targets, capacity, tasks, defects, rejections), washing (recipes,
             chemicals), finished goods (barcodes, shipment entries, delivery challans, pack lists),
             production floor (hourly/daily line output, workers, machines), QC (VistaQ inspections),
             dashboards and system administration (users, permissions, logins, report usage)
both       - needs a database value AND a document rule or comment
chitchat   - greeting, thanks, or a question about the assistant itself; no retrieval needed
refuse     - asks to change, approve, delete or send anything; or is unrelated to Bitopi's work

Rules: an EO number, FileRefID, style number, lot number, a buyer name with a date window, "how many",
"which orders", "status of", "stock", "PPM meeting", and any count, list, total or latest value of the
records above lean to data. "SOP", "policy", "procedure", "who approves", "rule", "form", "responsible",
"how do I", "buyer asked", "comment sheet", "tech pack" lean to documents. A "how many" question about
licences, laptops, desktops, mailboxes, servers, backups, retention, working days or anything governed
by an IT/HR policy is documents, not data — the count lives in a policy PDF; a "how many" about
employees, attendance, stock, vouchers, orders or production is data. If both kinds appear, answer both.
Output the label only, nothing else.
